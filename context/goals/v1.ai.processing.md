A provider sub-module pins only its own level, so a base module that depends on no tier stays usable below it (docproc's base, beside its `docproc/storage` and `docproc/postgres` providers over go-storage and go-database). repository-topology.md's "it depends on no tier" becomes this rule, and "Dependencies run downward" gains it; dependencies.md's "Bottom-up" gains it and "The line per tier"'s adjacent-library line becomes "never on a tier, unless the rule pins it above that tier, and a provider is a sub-module that pins its own"; topology-and-naming.md's "belongs to no tier" becomes "is a member of no tier", since a pinned level is not membership.
